Absolutely. If I were designing an **enterprise-level n8n automation portfolio**, I’d focus on workflows that solve repetitive business operations, reduce manual work, and connect tools like Gmail, Google Sheets, Slack, WhatsApp, CRMs, databases, AI, APIs, and cloud storage.

Here are **30 highly useful n8n automation workflows**, ordered roughly from common business automations to more advanced AI-powered systems.

### 1. Lead Capture → CRM Automation

Automatically collect leads from website forms, Facebook/Google forms, or APIs and create/update the lead in a CRM. Send an instant email/WhatsApp notification to the sales team.

### 2. Contact Form → Email + Google Sheets

When someone submits a website contact form, automatically save the information to Google Sheets and send a professional notification email to the business owner.

### 3. New Customer → Welcome Email

When a new customer registers, automatically create their customer record and send a personalized welcome email with useful onboarding information.

### 4. Email Attachment → Google Drive

Monitor Gmail for incoming attachments, automatically download them, organize them into appropriate Google Drive folders, and optionally notify the team.

### 5. Invoice Generation Automation

When an order is completed, collect customer/order information, generate an invoice PDF, store it in Google Drive, and email it to the customer.

### 6. Payment Success → Order Processing

When a payment gateway reports a successful payment, automatically update the order status, notify the customer, and trigger fulfillment/shipping processes.

### 7. Low Inventory Alert

Monitor inventory from Google Sheets, MySQL, PostgreSQL, or an API. When stock falls below a defined threshold, automatically notify the vendor/admin by email, Slack, or WhatsApp.

### 8. Daily Business Report

Every morning, fetch sales, orders, customers, revenue, and inventory information from different systems and generate a summarized report for management.

### 9. Employee Attendance Automation

Collect attendance data from an API, spreadsheet, or attendance system and automatically generate daily/weekly attendance reports for HR.

### 10. Customer Support Ticket Automation

When a support request arrives through email or a form, automatically create a ticket, categorize it, assign it to the appropriate team, and notify the responsible employee.

### 11. AI Email Classifier

Use an AI model to analyze incoming emails and classify them as **Sales, Support, Complaint, Billing, Urgent, Spam**, etc. Then route each email to the correct workflow.

### 12. AI Email Auto-Reply

Analyze incoming customer emails with AI, generate a context-aware response, and either send it automatically or send it to an employee for approval before sending.

### 13. WhatsApp Customer Notification

Automatically send WhatsApp messages for events such as order confirmation, payment success, delivery updates, appointment reminders, or cancellations.

### 14. Order → Delivery Tracking

When an order is created, automatically create a delivery task and send status updates to the customer whenever the delivery status changes.

### 15. Appointment Booking + Reminder

When an appointment is booked, automatically save it to Google Calendar, send confirmation to the customer, and send reminder messages before the appointment.

### 16. Abandoned Cart Recovery

Detect customers who added products to their cart but didn't complete the purchase. After a defined period, automatically send reminder emails or WhatsApp messages.

### 17. Customer Feedback Automation

After an order/service is completed, automatically send a feedback request. Store the response and notify the team if the customer gives a poor rating.

### 18. Negative Review Alert

Monitor reviews from supported platforms/APIs. If a negative review is detected, use AI to analyze the complaint and immediately notify the appropriate team.

### 19. Social Media Content Automation

Take content from a Google Sheet or content database, generate captions using AI, prepare platform-specific content, and publish or schedule it through connected APIs.

### 20. AI Blog Content Pipeline

Provide a topic → AI generates an outline → generates the article → creates metadata → stores the draft in Google Docs/Notion → sends it for human approval.

### 21. Document → AI Summary

Whenever a new PDF/document is uploaded to a specific folder, automatically extract its content, summarize it using AI, and save the summary to Notion/Google Docs.

### 22. Resume Screening Automation

When a candidate uploads a resume, extract the resume text, use AI to compare it against a job description, score the candidate, and store the result in a recruitment database.

### 23. Invoice Data Extraction

When an invoice PDF arrives by email, extract vendor name, invoice number, date, tax, total amount, etc. using OCR/AI and automatically store the structured data in a database or accounting sheet.

### 24. Employee Onboarding Automation

When HR adds a new employee, automatically create their records, generate onboarding tasks, send required documents, create accounts/tasks, and notify the relevant departments.

### 25. Employee Offboarding Automation

When an employee leaves, automatically create an offboarding checklist, notify HR/IT, revoke required access through APIs, archive documents, and inform management.

### 26. Database Backup Automation

Run scheduled database backups, store backup files in cloud storage, verify that the backup completed successfully, and notify administrators if anything fails.

### 27. API Monitoring + Failure Alert

Periodically call important APIs/services and verify response time/status. If an API fails or returns unexpected data, automatically alert the developer/admin through Slack, email, or WhatsApp.

### 28. AI Customer Support Agent

Connect WhatsApp/email/webhook messages to an AI agent. The AI understands the customer's question, searches your knowledge base/database, and responds automatically while escalating complex issues to humans.

### 29. AI Business Data Analyst

Connect n8n to your business database. A user can ask questions such as **"What were our top 10 products this month?"** and the workflow converts the question into database queries, analyzes the result with AI, and returns a human-readable answer.

### 30. Complete AI Business Automation Agent

Build a central AI agent that can understand business requests and execute actions through n8n—for example: **check inventory → find customer → create invoice → send email → update CRM → notify employee**. This becomes a powerful internal AI operations assistant rather than a single-purpose workflow.
